# Michi-Maker or Woahpoke's Michi Method Template?

*By Brian, who builds Michi-Maker. Written November 15, 2026. I have a stake in this, so read
Woahpoke's page too.*

If you search for the michi method, Woahpoke's Michi Method Template is one of the first tools
you meet, and it is a good one. It does a narrower job than Michi-Maker, and for that job it may
be the better pick. [BRIAN: one line on how you came across it, or delete.]

## What each one does

**Woahpoke's Michi Method Template** (their page, 2026-11-15):

- Free, in the browser, no account or sign-up.
- Pick a pocket layout (9, 12, 8 or 4 pockets, rows and columns adjustable), merge pockets, upload
  an image and crop it across them.
- Print settings: paper size, piece size, calibration, cutting marks and labels; exports a PDF of
  print-ready pieces, a PNG of the page, and a project file.
- Projects stay in your browser; the page says nothing is uploaded to a server.
- You can add cards from a database (TCGdex) to plan around.

**Michi-Maker** (as of 2026-11-15):

- Free in the browser; you can start without an account. Binders are saved to your account, so
  they follow you between devices once you have one, and can be shared as a link.
- Builds the page **around the cards**: put one card in a pocket and fill the page by Pokémon,
  evolution line, artist or colour, or search by what the artwork shows.
- Slices a picture across pockets too, and prints true-size fill sheets (63 by 88 mm).

## Pick Woahpoke's template if

- Your page is mostly **your own artwork** cut into pockets, and you want the most control over
  the print: calibration, cutting marks, labels, paper size.
- You want **nothing stored on a server** at all.
- You need **8-pocket** pages.
- You want one page done in a few minutes with no account, ever.

## Pick Michi-Maker if

- Your page is mostly **cards**, and you want help choosing them: which Gengar cards, which
  cards share this colour, what else this artist drew.
- You are planning **a whole binder**, page after page, and want to keep it and share it.
