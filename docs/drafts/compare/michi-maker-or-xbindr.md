# Michi-Maker or xBindr?

*By Brian, who builds Michi-Maker. Written November 15, 2026. I have a stake in this; check
xBindr's site yourself before you decide.*

xBindr and Michi-Maker are the closest of the binder tools: both are browser binder makers, both
do michi-style pages, both print. The differences are in the details, and some of them favour
xBindr. [BRIAN: one sentence on how you know xBindr, or delete.]

## What each one does

**xBindr** (their homepage, 2026-11-15):

- Binder maker in the browser with drag and drop between pages, grids from 2×2 to 5×4.
- More than 97,500 cards in English, French and Japanese, every printing selectable.
- Color Match, extended art across a page, and michi-method pages filled with your own artwork.
- Binder looks: grain, leather, canvas, gradient or solid.
- Prints at card or pocket size on A4 or Letter, or saves a PDF.
- Prices from both Cardmarket (euros) and TCGplayer (dollars), with a binder total.
- Free: 5 binders, up to 1,100 cards each. Premium: $25 every 6 months, or monthly; adds
  unlimited binders, your own images and the slicing tool. Riftbound is supported; One Piece is
  "Soon".

**Michi-Maker** (as of 2026-11-15):

- Binder maker in the browser; you can start without an account.
- Fill a page around one card by Pokémon, evolution line, artist or colour, or search cards by
  what the artwork shows.
- Set binders in number order, and set planners that count pages and sheets per binder size.
- English and Japanese cards (an EN / JP switch on Browse and the home feed). Chinese, French and
  Pokémon TCG Pocket are planned, no date yet.
- Prints true-size fill sheets (63 by 88 mm).
- Free account: 2 binders of up to 20 pages. Paid plan: $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year, no page or
  binder limits, plus binder covers and unlimited artwork search.

## Pick xBindr if

- You collect **French** cards, or price in **euros** from Cardmarket: xBindr covers both today.
  Michi-Maker has English and Japanese cards and TCGplayer prices; French is planned, no date yet.
- You want **more free room**: 5 binders of up to 1,100 cards beats our free 2 binders of 20 pages.
- You want **grid sizes beyond 4×4**, or binder materials like leather and canvas.
- You want to slice and print **your own images** and are happy on their Premium plan.

## Pick Michi-Maker if

- You want help **filling** a page, not just arranging one: start from one card and let the page
  come together around it.
- You want to search cards by **what the artwork shows** (a beach, a night sky, food).
- You want to try it **without signing up**.
