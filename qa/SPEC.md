## How to read a row

- **proves** is what the row protects, in plain words. If a row fails and you cannot tell why it mattered, that field is the bug.
- **A dash** in a tier column means the check makes no claim at that tier. An empty column is a coverage hole and is meant to be visible.
- **pro-trial** holds `tier_pro` and `tcgscan_pro`, the same products a paid PRO holds, so every PRO gate resolves identically. There is no `pro` or `vip` column: a paid tier needs a live charge or the management token, and the trial is close enough (owner, 2026-09-21).
- **unreachable** is an expectation, not a skip. The check FAILS if the thing turns out to be reachable.
- **danger** is what the check writes. The console sums it into a write budget above the Run button.

## Gates and caps

The three free binder pages at every step of exhaustion, and every paid gate asserted from BOTH sides: locked for guest and free, open for a PRO trial. A lock nobody has seen open is not a tested gate.

### `tcgscan.gating.binder-lock-copy-guest`

**A guest tapping binder mode is asked to sign in, not asked to pay**

Guests get zero free binder pages on purpose: a guest has no account for a binder to sync to, so the sign-in prompt is the right next step and a paywall is the wrong one. A refactor that treats guest as "free with 0 left" would show the paywall and nobody would notice.
