## 1. Instagram Story  (`puzzle-0924-story.png`, 2000x3556)

**Not the quiz sticker.** A quiz has to print its options on the screen, and printing four words one
of which is the answer is most of the puzzle given away. It also only marks one choice correct, so
it cannot take a two-theme answer at all.

Two stickers in the empty band instead:

- **Questions sticker** ("Guess the two themes"). Free text, nothing given away, and the responses
  come to you in a private list rather than into public view, so no one spoils it for the next
  viewer. Good guesses can be re-shared as follow-up frames, which is another day of content for
  nothing.
- **Link sticker** to the daily puzzle on the site.

**The raffle entry is the site, not the sticker.** Answering on michi-maker is graded automatically
(`puzzle_plays.correct`), is tied to an account, and needs no one to read anything. The Questions
sticker is there for the people who will never leave Instagram; the link is for the ones who will.
That way the draw is a database query on Monday morning rather than an afternoon of reading replies,
and every entrant is a signed-up account rather than a handle.

A countdown sticker to the next puzzle works well on the reveal frame the following morning.

Story text, if any, goes ABOVE the page. The band under it must stay clear or the stickers land on
the reply bar.
